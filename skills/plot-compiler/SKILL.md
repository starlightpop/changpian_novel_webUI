# plot-compiler

## Purpose

Use after the master outline and character system are approved to design volume plans and detailed chapter outlines for a long-form webnovel.

## First Principles

1. Every event must be caused by character goals, choices, faction plans, resource limits, world rules, or consequences of earlier events.
2. Every major result must change at least one tracked state: information, relationship, resource, position, power, reputation, risk, or reader expectation.
3. No new character, item, ability, clue, location, or solution may appear without registration, origin, acquisition, and prior setup.
4. A climax must consume prior setup. A payoff without a seed is invalid.
5. Ensemble threads must have independent drivers and must intersect through causal events, not arbitrary coincidence.

## Workflow

1. Compile the approved master outline into volume-level start and end state deltas.
2. Define three-act multi-thread architecture, including thread owners and intersections.
3. Generate chapter cards in small batches.
4. Validate chapter numbering, registered characters, time and space continuity, backward-only prerequisites, clue seeds and payoffs, and required state changes.
5. Audit pacing, suspense, reversals, emotional highs and lows, plausibility, ensemble balance, and ending hooks.
6. Submit semantically imperfect but structurally valid results for human review instead of blocking forever on a score.

## Chapter Card

Each chapter must include:

- chapter number, volume, title
- time, location, viewpoint, participants
- narrative thread and prerequisite chapters
- opening state
- character goal and active choice
- opposition and motive
- conflict and plot summary
- turn, cost, state delta
- emotional curve
- planted and paid clues
- ending hook

## Quality Gate

- Program hard failures block submission.
- LLM quality scores below the automatic threshold require human review.
- Never lower standards or fabricate a passing score.
