# novel-final-audit

## Purpose

Use after the master outline, character system, relationship graph, and chapter blueprint have passed their own deterministic gates.

## Evidence Priority

1. User-confirmed facts and the story constitution.
2. Character canon contracts and confirmed event cards.
3. Approved master outline and faction plans.
4. Approved chapter cards and promise ledger.
5. Agent inference.

Never resolve an upstream conflict by choosing a convenient version. Report it and repair the owning source first.

## Workflow

1. Freeze the canonical context fingerprint and model route.
2. Run deterministic cross-domain validation before semantic review.
3. Audit character agency, arc realization, relationship progression, causal continuity, time and space continuity, resource provenance, ensemble balance, pacing, suspense, and clue payoff.
4. Return field-level patches with evidence. Do not regenerate whole characters or chapters.
5. Apply patches transactionally and rerun all affected deterministic checks.
6. Roll back any patch that introduces a new severe issue.
7. Submit the candidate for human approval. Do not write directly to formal facts.

## Hard Blockers

- Unknown or placeholder characters
- Ghost relationships
- Events before a character's registered appearance
- Unregistered people, items, abilities, locations, or solutions
- Payoffs without earlier seeds
- Fate, identity, faction, timeline, or story-constitution conflicts
- Forbidden concepts or style violations

## Output Contract

Return a score, concise summary, evidence-backed issues, strengths, minimal patches, and a final integrated outline. Program validation decides structural acceptance; the model score alone never overrides a hard blocker.
